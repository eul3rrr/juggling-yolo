01 / THE PROBLEM

A detector sees moments.
Analysis needs continuity.

Frame-based detection and local tracking are good at answering “what is here now?” Hands, occlusion, blur, and low confidence still split one physical ball into short local tracklets.

07 / NEXT STEPS

Where the research
goes next.

The current hand path is one repair mechanism, not the finished juggling model.

persistent ball trajectories-> throw / catch events-> siteswap inference-> JML encoding-> machine-readable patterns
01

Airborne repair

Use motion and ballistic evidence to connect fragments outside the hand. Experimental, not shown here as a prediction.

02

Body occlusion

Handle torso and limb occlusions with explicit body-aware evidence instead of silently interpolating.

03

Siteswap inference

Infer throw structure and juggling pattern notation from reconstructed trajectories and explicit throw/catch events.

04

JML encoding

Encode inferred sequences in Juggling Markup Language (JML), a machine-readable format for juggling patterns.

05

Live prototype

A webcam path exists as a separate prototype and active development path; it is not exercised or presented as complete in this offline demo.
