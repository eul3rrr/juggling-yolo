Survey definitions

Hand overlap measures actual hand pixels over the physical ball silhouette, not proximity.

  • none: no hand overlap
  • slight: small overlap; most ball visible
  • moderate: substantial overlap, clearly identifiable
  • heavy: most covered, some real ball pixels remain

Visibility: clear = full/almost full; partial = part visible; tiny_fragment = very little genuine surface visible. Independent of occluder.

Blur: none / mild / strong, judged visually. Confidence: certain / uncertain. Use uncertain when hidden extent cannot be estimated confidently.
